Automated Background SMS & Webhook Sync

Hands-free background ingestion directly from your phone over local Wi-Fi
Active & Listening

FinTrack runs a silent background webhook on your machine. When your bank sends an SMS or email debit alert to your phone, free Android forwarder apps push the notification text here over your home Wi-Fi. It is automatically parsed, categorized, and logged into SQLite with zero manual copy-pasting.

Step 1: Connect to Wi-Fi
Ensure your phone and this computer are connected to the same Wi-Fi router / local network (IP: {{ local_ip }}).
Step 2: Phone Forwarder App
Install free SMS Forwarder or MacroDroid on Android. Set rule: on SMS from Bank, POST JSON {"text": "%body%"} to Webhook URL.
Step 3: Auto-Captured & Saved
FinTrack extracts amount, merchant, and category, detects duplicates, and auto-commits to SQLite silently in the background!
🧪 You can also test the webhook directly from terminal: python backend/send_test_sms.py
